Luca Arnaboldi

Researcher in Verifiably Secure ML Systems

University of Birmingham, School of Computer Science

Assistant Professor Founder & Director Consultant Speaker

I am a researcher and educator specializing in the security of autonomous systems and the formal verification of machine learning algorithms, with a strong focus on delivering real-world impact through innovation and industry engagement. My work has contributed to internationally adopted tools and has been reported on by multiple global media outlets. I have secured over £1.3 million in competitive grants to bridge academic research and practical applications.

If you are interested in any of these topics feel free to contact me at l.arnaboldi@bham.ac.uk
